# Engineering review checklist

- [ ] Change traces to an accepted PRD, applicable ADRs, and one EPKG SPEC.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are met without expanding scope or introducing speculative abstractions.
- [ ] Tests cover changed behavior and every required quality gate has factual evidence.
- [ ] Security, privacy, dependency, compatibility, and operational effects were considered.
- [ ] Canonical terminology and policy are linked rather than duplicated.
- [ ] Metadata, navigation, and relative Markdown links are correct.
- [ ] Full diff contains no contradiction, secret, unfinished marker, inaccessible context, or unrelated edit.
- [ ] Residual risks and deferred decisions name an owner or future decision path.
