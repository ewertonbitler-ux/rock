# PRD-002: Engagement Context

## Product outcome

Introduce the Engagement Context as Rocky's first proposed
[Engineering Domain](../glossary.md). Rocky can represent an engineering engagement through a
canonical, governed business boundary while repository artifacts remain the source of product and
architecture authority under [RKY-000](../charters/RKY-000.md).

## Users and needs

- **Maintainer:** establish and govern an engagement without coupling its meaning to a delivery
  tool, storage mechanism, or provider.
- **Contributor:** understand an engagement's ownership, participants, lifecycle position, and
  governing engineering intent.
- **Reviewer:** trace engagement behavior and outcomes to the Rocky artifacts that authorize them.
- **Domain integrator:** consume a bounded public contract without reaching into domain internals or
  transferring domain ownership to another context.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PRD-002-R1 | Engagement has one canonical, technology-neutral representation of an engineering engagement. | The future EPKG-0004 specifies one coherent Engagement boundary and rejects competing representations. |
| PRD-002-R2 | Engagement behavior follows a governed lifecycle whose transitions protect business validity. | The future EPKG-0004 defines and verifies lifecycle rules derived from this PRD without changing repository lifecycle policy. |
| PRD-002-R3 | An engagement identifies accountable ownership and its participants. | The future EPKG-0004 specifies ownership and participation requirements with observable validation evidence. |
| PRD-002-R4 | Engagement business policy remains independent of persistence, infrastructure, external providers, and delivery tools. | Architecture checks and review evidence demonstrate inward dependencies and provider-neutral behavior. |
| PRD-002-R5 | Engagement publishes only the bounded contracts required by future Engineering Domains and consumers. | The future EPKG-0004 identifies each justified public contract and confirms domain internals remain private. |
| PRD-002-R6 | An engagement is traceable to the authoritative Rocky product, architecture, package, and evidence artifacts that govern it. | Review evidence maps the delivered behavior to stable repository artifact identifiers and relative links. |

## Non-functional requirements

The Engagement Context must use concise, provider-neutral business language and preserve stable
traceability. Its domain policy must be deterministic, testable without external services, and
independent of storage and deployment choices. Public contracts must be minimal, explicit, and
compatible with Rocky's inward dependency rule. Repository knowledge remains canonical and must
not be duplicated into domain policy.

## Out of scope

This PRD does not define Python classes, aggregate internals, value-object structures, repository
interfaces, exact lifecycle states, method signatures, persistence, APIs, events, infrastructure,
deployment, user interfaces, external integrations, or AI orchestration. It does not define other
Engineering Domains or change the independent Knowledge Asset domain. Detailed domain modeling and
delivery are governed later by EPKG-0004, which is not created by this proposal.

## Release decision

Engagement is not approved, delivered, or implemented by this proposed PRD. Release requires
maintainer acceptance of this PRD, acceptance of any consequential architecture decisions, a
separately reviewed EPKG-0004 that defines the detailed domain model, complete requirement evidence,
and all repository quality gates passing. Until then, Engagement remains proposed for specification.
