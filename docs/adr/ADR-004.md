# ADR-004: Introduce Engineering Domains

## Context

Rocky began as a repository-native engineering framework and now includes an independent,
persistence-free Knowledge Asset domain. Repository artifacts remain the durable authority for
product intent, architecture, delivery guidance, and evidence. Rocky needs a minimal architectural
basis for adding executable engineering behavior without displacing that authority, coupling
engineering policy to infrastructure, or creating a speculative taxonomy of domain types.

[PRD-002](../prd/PRD-002.md) approves Engagement for specification. Because introducing executable
engineering concepts alongside the foundational Knowledge concern is consequential and cross-cutting,
the architectural boundary requires an explicit decision before implementation can be authorized.
This ADR establishes that architectural boundary without authorizing implementation.

## Decision

Rocky will use **Engineering Domain** for a bounded model of governed engineering
concepts, policies, and behavior. Knowledge remains a foundational concern, and Knowledge Asset
remains a valid, independent domain concept. Engagement will be the first Engineering Domain and,
under its accepted governing PRD, is approved only for specification.

Repository artifacts remain the source of product and architecture authority. Engineering Domains
implement governed behavior through bounded published contracts; they do not replace repository
governance or own the artifacts that authorize them. Domain code must remain independent of
persistence, infrastructure, delivery tools, external providers, and provider-specific intelligence.
Intelligence and AI capabilities may consume published domain contracts but must not own domain
policy or behavior.

Each new Engineering Domain requires an accepted PRD and a dedicated EPKG before implementation.
An additional ADR is required only when that domain introduces a consequential, cross-cutting, or
difficult-to-reverse architecture decision. This decision introduces no permanent taxonomy beyond
the Engineering Domain term and makes no choice about domain internals, persistence, interfaces,
APIs, infrastructure, or deployment.

## Consequences

Rocky can evolve from repository-native governance toward executable engineering behavior while
keeping intent reviewable and provider-neutral. Knowledge Asset and Engagement can evolve through
independent boundaries, and future consumers depend on explicit contracts rather than domain
internals.

Each Engineering Domain incurs product and package review before implementation. Consequential
architecture still requires acceptance through the existing ADR lifecycle. Maintainers must guard
against duplicated repository authority, infrastructure leakage, overly broad public contracts,
and premature domain classification. This decision authorizes specification of Engagement but no
implementation; a separately reviewed and accepted EPKG is still required.

## Alternatives considered

- **Remain documentation-only:** preserves the original boundary but cannot support governed,
  executable engineering behavior proposed by PRD-002.
- **Treat all executable concepts as Knowledge Assets:** reuses an existing model but conflates
  repository knowledge with distinct engineering behavior and weakens independent ownership.
- **Define a comprehensive domain taxonomy now:** may appear systematic, but existing requirements
  do not justify stable categories beyond Engineering Domain.
- **Let AI or integration layers own domain behavior:** accelerates a provider-specific path but
  reverses the dependency rule and makes engineering policy volatile.
